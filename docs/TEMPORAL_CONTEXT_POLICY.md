# MAGI temporal and historical weighting policy

- Every deliberation must receive the current date and time in Asia/Tokyo.
- Relative expressions such as 半年後, 来夏, 来年, 今季 are resolved from that timestamp.
- For 2026-2027 lineup/candidate decisions, current-team evidence is primary.
- 2025-2026 evidence remains important historical evidence when sample size and role continuity are substantial.
- In particular, 大久保 陽翔 and 大野 竜暉 had substantial old-team playing time; their 2025-2026 record is evidence of game experience and reproducibility and must not be treated as a negligible reference.
- 大久保 陽翔's established cleanup/4th-hitter continuity and team-pillar role must be considered as role-continuity context, while still allowing current evidence to justify a change.
- Small-sample old-team records for players who were not regulars must not be used against their current evaluation.
- Current-team roster for the 2026-2027 review is 14 players; all-player review must check exactly the authoritative roster, not every name that happens to appear in a 2026-2027-labelled file.
